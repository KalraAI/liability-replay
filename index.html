09 - How it works

No video needed.

Venues already record what happens: camera events, door logs, till times, alarms. The replay is built from data the venue already has. Video helps, but it's not required.

It learns from synthetic incidents.

Synthetic slips, fights, break-ins and staged claims, each with a known answer. We write the cases, then score the system against them.

Sealed records, not stories.

Every record is fingerprinted, time-stamped and written to an audit log. You get a record you can check, not a story you have to piece together again.

10 - What this is and isn't

Liability Replay is in beta. It replays the data you give it and shows what's missing. A person makes the call. It does not decide legal notice or liability.

Real-world numbers (public data)
